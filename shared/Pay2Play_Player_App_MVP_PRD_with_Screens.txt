Pay2Play Player App – MVP PRD 
1. Product Overview 
Product: Pay2Play – Player App (MVP) 
Platform: Expo React Native (iOS, Android, Web) 
Market (Phase 1): Lahore, Pakistan (Cricket, Futsal/Football, Padel) 
The Player App is the consumer-facing experience of Pay2Play: it lets players discover vetted venues, see real-time availability, book and pay digitally, find teammates/opponents, and join leagues/events—while enjoying visible discounts funded by venue promotions. 
The MVP focuses on the fastest possible journey from “I want to play” to “booking confirmed”, targeted at “book a pitch in under 60 seconds” from home screen to success state. 
2. Goals & Non-Goals 
2.1 Goals (MVP) 
1. Let a new player: 
○ Download/open the app, 
○ Complete onboarding, 
○ Discover a venue, 
○ See real-time slots, 
○ Book & pay, 
○ See clear confirmation + discount, 
○ All in < 60 seconds under ideal network conditions.
2. Enable basic social play: 
○ Players can join host-led matches that need extra players. 
○ Players can post simple LFP/LFG posts (no advanced matchmaking yet). 3. Establish trust & retention hooks: 
○ Transparent venue info, pricing, and available time slots. 
○ Display of discount and money saved per booking. 
○ Basic loyalty & rewards currency (credits) earned via play and referrals. 
2.2 Non-Goals (Not in this MVP) 
● Split payments between teammates. 
● Dynamic pricing rules engine for venues. 
● Weather & AI-based attendance prediction. 
● Full tournament management, marketplace, or AI matchmaking. 
These are planned for later phases. 
3. Target Users & Personas 
3.1 Casual Captain (“Organizer Player”) 
● Age 18–35, organizes weekly 5-a-side or tape-ball games. 
● Needs: fast ground booking, reliable slots, easy way to fill missing players. ● Key flows: Onboarding → Venue Discovery → Booking → Host Match → Invite/Share. 
3.2 Regular Player (“Joiner”)
● Age 16–35, joins existing games more than organizing them. ● Needs: visibility of open games, simple join flow, in-app chat. ● Key flows: Onboarding → Player Matching → Join Host-Led Match → Pay. 
4. Core Experience & Navigation 
4.1 App Navigation Structure (MVP) 
Bottom tab bar (4–5 tabs): 
1. Home 
○ Smart shortcuts: “Book a pitch”, “Join a match tonight”. 
○ Highlighted venues and open matches. 
2. Venues 
○ Venue Discovery, search, filters, and venue detail. 
3. Matches 
○ Player Matching feed (LFP/LFG) + Host-led Gameplay list. 4. Messages 
○ Conversation list and chat threads. 
5. Profile 
○ Player Profile, rewards & referrals, booking history, settings. 5. Feature Specifications
For each feature, I’ll cover: User stories → Functional requirements → UX & visualization notes → Analytics & edge cases. 
5.1 Onboarding 
5.1.1 User Stories 
● As a new player, I want to quickly set up my basic preferences so the app shows relevant venues and matches. 
● As a returning player, I want to skip unnecessary steps and go directly to the home screen. 
5.1.2 Functional Requirements 
1. First-run detection 
○ Detect first app launch vs logged-in user. 
2. Auth 
○ MVP: Phone number + OTP (primary), optionally social (Google/Apple) if ready. 3. Data collected during onboarding 
○ Name and display handle (e.g. “Abdullah F.”). 
○ Primary location: city (Lahore) and area/neighborhood. 
○ Preferred sports (multi-select: Cricket, Football/Futsal, Padel). 
○ Skill level (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced). 
○ Time preference (Evenings / Weekends / Flexible). 
4. Consent & policies 
○ Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy acceptance. 
5. Onboarding completion
○ Persist profile data. 
○ Navigate to Home. 
5.1.3 UX / Visualization 
Screens: 
1. Welcome Screen 
○ Branding, tagline: “Pay less, play more” / “Book a pitch in under 60 seconds”. ○ Buttons: “Continue with Phone” (primary). 
2. Phone Verification 
○ Phone input → OTP verification screen. 
3. Basic Info 
○ Form with Name, Avatar selection (preset avatars) and preferred sport chips. 4. Preferences 
○ Select city/area (dropdown or typeahead). 
○ Sport, skill level, time preference (radio buttons or segment controls). 5. Review & Continue 
○ Short summary and “Finish & Go to Home”. 
5.2 Player Profiles 
5.2.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want a profile with my preferred sports and avatar so other players know what I play. 
● As a host, I want to see basic info on players joining my match.
5.2.2 Functional Requirements 
● Store: 
○ Name, avatar, city/area. 
○ Preferred sports list + primary sport. 
○ Skill level per sport (optional MVP: one global level). 
○ Short bio (max 160 chars). 
● Profile editing: 
○ Editable from Profile tab. 
● View: 
○ Public mini-profile (when tapping a player in match participants or chat). 
5.2.3 UX / Visualization 
Profile Screen (tab) 
● Header: Avatar, name, primary sport, city. 
● Sections: 
○ About: Bio, skill level. 
○ Sports: Sport chips with icons. 
○ Stats (stub for future): “Games played, win rate” placeholders (greyed or “Coming soon”). 
○ Bookings: Shortcut to booking history. 
○ Rewards: See current credits balance and referral link. 
5.3 Venue Discovery
5.3.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to discover vetted venues near me by sport, area, and amenities. 
5.3.2 Functional Requirements 
● Venue List: 
○ Data: name, sport(s), location, price per slot/hour, rating (if available), key amenities (lights, parking, showers), thumbnail image, discount badge (if active). 
● Search: 
○ Search by venue name, area, or keyword. 
● Filters: 
○ Sport, location/area, price range, indoor/outdoor, “has discount”. 
● Sorting: 
○ Recommended (default), Price (low–high), Distance (if location available). 
5.3.3 UX / Visualization 
Venues Tab: 
● Top: Search bar + filter icon. 
● Underneath: Horizontal chips (Cricket, Football, Padel). 
● Venue cards: 
○ Image banner. 
○ Name + area. 
○ Price from (e.g., “From PKR 5,000 / hour”). 
○ Discount pill: “Save up to 20%”. 
○ Rating (stars) and number of reviews (when available).
Venue Detail Screen: 
● Header: Image carousel, venue name, area, rating. 
● Tabs/sections: 
1. About: Description, amenities list, house rules. 
2. Availability: Date selector + time slot grid (see next section). 
3. Location: Map or address + directions link. 
5.4 Real-time Availability 
5.4.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I need to see which slots are actually free so I can confidently book. ● As a venue, I want a single source of truth to avoid double bookings. 
5.4.2 Functional Requirements 
● Date selector (today + next 7–14 days). 
● Slots: 
○ Fetch real-time slot availability from backend for chosen sport, date, and pitch/court (if multiple). 
○ Statuses: Available, Booked, Unavailable (greyed), Past (disabled). ● Slot duration: 
○ Configurable per venue (e.g., 60 or 90 minutes). 
● Time zone: Local (PKT). 
5.4.3 UX / Visualization 
In Venue Detail → Availability:
● Horizontal date strip (Today, Wed 26, Thu 27…). 
● Beneath: segmented control to choose pitch/court if venue has multiple. ● Grid-style slot display: 
○ Pill buttons with time range (e.g., “7:00–8:00 PM”). 
○ Color coding: 
■ Primary for Available. 
■ Grey for Booked/Unavailable. 
● Selecting a slot leads to Booking Review screen. 
5.5 Booking Engine 
5.5.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to confirm details (date, time, price, discount) before paying. ● As a player, I want to see and manage my upcoming and past bookings. 
5.5.2 Functional Requirements 
1. Booking creation 
○ Inputs: venue, sport, pitch (if any), date, time slot, number of players (approx.), price, discount applied, final amount. 
○ API call to create pending booking before payment; confirmed after successful payment. 
2. Booking status 
○ Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, Refunded. 
3. Booking history
○ List of upcoming and past bookings for the user. 
4. Cancellation 
○ MVP: simple cancellation rule (e.g., free cancellation until X hours before; beyond that, non-refundable or partial) aligning with existing refund engine. 
5.5.3 UX / Visualization 
Booking Review Screen: 
● Card with: 
○ Venue, sport, pitch. 
○ Date and time slot. 
○ Price breakdown: 
■ Base price 
■ Discount (“You save PKR X thanks to Pay2Play”) 
■ Payable amount. 
● Bottom CTA: “Pay & Book”. 
My Bookings Screen (under Profile or separate entry): 
● Tabs: Upcoming | Past. 
● Booking cards displaying: 
○ Venue, sport icon, date/time. 
○ Status chip (Confirmed, Cancelled). 
● Tapping card: Booking Detail with map, rules, cancellation button (if allowed), “Contact venue” and “Open chat”.
5.6 Digital Payments 
5.6.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to pay seamlessly via local payment methods and see a clear confirmation. 
5.6.2 Functional Requirements 
● Integrations (MVP): 
○ JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank card gateway (or Stripe for card). 
● Payment flows: 
○ Initiate payment from Booking Review. 
○ Show loading/success/failure states. 
● Wallet / credits: 
○ Show available credits (loyalty) that can be applied to reduce payable amount. ● Receipts: 
○ Store transaction reference and display on Booking Confirmation and Booking Detail. 
5.6.3 UX / Visualization 
Payment Screen: 
● Order summary at top. 
● Payment options list: 
○ “Pay with JazzCash” 
○ “Pay with Easypaisa” 
○ “Pay with Card”
● Toggle: “Apply X credits (save PKR Y)” when loyalty is present. 
● After success: 
○ Booking Success Screen with confetti animation, quick share CTA: “Share booking with your team”. 
5.7 Discount Display 
5.7.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to clearly see how much I am saving so that I feel the value of using Pay2Play. 
5.7.2 Functional Requirements 
● Per booking: 
○ Show full venue price vs discounted price. 
○ Show “You saved PKR X” line item. 
● On venue and search cards: 
○ Show discount badge or highlight (e.g., “Up to 20% off today”). 
● Hook into backend discount pool logic. 
5.7.3 UX / Visualization 
● Venue cards: small colored pill “Save up to 20%”. 
● Booking Review/Payment: line item “Discount (Pay2Play offer)” and “You saved PKR X”. ● Booking Success: highlight text “You saved PKR X on this game”. 
5.8 Player Matching (LFP/LFG Posts)
5.8.1 User Stories 
● As a host, I want to create a post saying I’m looking for players for a booked slot. 
● As a player, I want to see nearby games where teams are missing players and join easily. 
5.8.2 Functional Requirements 
1. Create LFP/LFG Post 
○ Link to an existing booking OR generic “looking for ground + players” (MVP: strongly encourage linking to a booking). 
○ Fields: 
■ Sport, date, time, venue (if fixed), skill level, required positions/roles, number of players needed. 
2. Match Feed 
○ List posts filtered by: 
■ Sport, date range, area, skill level. 
3. Join Flow 
○ Player taps “Request to Join”. 
○ Host gets a notification and can accept/decline. 
4. Participant list 
○ For each match, show number of confirmed players and their mini-profiles. 
5.8.3 UX / Visualization 
Matches Tab: 
● Top: Sport filter chips + date filter. 
● Cards:
○ Title: “5-a-side game needs 2 players”. 
○ Subtitle: Venue/Area + time. 
○ Chips: Skill level, sport. 
○ CTA: “Request to Join”. 
● Detail Screen: 
○ Host info, slots, list of confirmed players, “Chat with Host” button. 
5.9 Leagues & Events 
5.9.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to discover leagues and tournaments and register from the app. 
5.9.2 Functional Requirements 
● Basic list of upcoming leagues/events: 
○ Fields: title, sport, organizer name, venue(s), dates, registration fee, registration deadline. 
● Registration: 
○ MVP: simple “Register Interest” or “External Link” (to WhatsApp/website) if full payment integration for leagues isn’t ready. 
● Event detail: 
○ Description, schedule overview, rules, link to contact organizer. 
5.9.3 UX / Visualization 
● Section on Home & Matches: 
○ Horizontal carousel: “Upcoming Leagues & Events”.
● Event Detail Screen: 
○ Hero banner, title, sports icons. 
○ Date range, location, fee. 
○ CTA: “Register” (in-app or external). 
5.10 In-app Messaging 
5.10.1 User Stories 
● As a host, I want to coordinate with players who joined my match. ● As a player, I want to ask venue-specific questions (e.g., ball provided?). 
5.10.2 Functional Requirements 
● Conversation types: 
○ Player ↔ Player (around matches). 
○ Player ↔ Venue (booking-related). 
● Message basics: 
○ Text messages, timestamps, read status (simple). 
○ Push notifications for new messages. 
● Linking: 
○ Chat thread associated with a booking or match. 
5.10.3 UX / Visualization 
Messages Tab: 
● List of conversations:
○ Avatar (user or venue logo), name, last message snippet, timestamp. ● Chat Screen: 
○ Standard bubble layout, sticky header with opponent name and linked booking/match info. 
5.11 Loyalty & Rewards 
5.11.1 User Stories 
● As a player, I want to earn credits when I play and refer friends, and later redeem them to pay less. 
5.11.2 Functional Requirements 
● Credits: 
○ Earn rules (MVP simple): 
■ X credits per completed booking. 
■ Y credits when a referral completes their first booking. 
○ Redeem rules: 
■ 1 credit = 1 PKR discount (or other conversion). 
■ Max % of booking payable with credits (e.g., 30%). 
● Balance: 
○ Display on Profile and Payment screen. 
● Referral: 
○ Each user gets a referral link/code. 
○ Track installs and first booking with that code.
5.11.3 UX / Visualization 
Rewards Section (Profile): 
● Current balance: “You have 450 credits”. 
● Recent activity: “+200 from last game at XYZ”, “+250 from inviting Ali”. ● Button: “Invite friends” → share sheet. 
Payment screen shows toggle: “Apply 200 credits (save PKR 200)”. 
5.12 Organizers / Host-led Gameplay 
(Tightly coupled with Player Matching & Booking) 
5.12.1 User Stories 
● As a host (player or organizer), I want to list my match so others can join. ● As a player, I want to scroll a feed of host-led games and join with one tap. 
5.12.2 Functional Requirements 
● Create Host-led Match: 
○ Either: 
■ From a confirmed booking: “List this booking as open game”. 
■ Or from Matches tab: create generic host-led game (MVP: strongly prefer linking to booking). 
● Fields: 
○ Maximum players, open slots, cost per player (informational), rules (e.g., “no studs on turf”). 
● Joining: 
○ When player is accepted, show:
■ Match in “Joined Matches” section. 
■ Payment expectations (if host collects money offline vs via app; MVP: show offline cash note). 
5.12.3 UX / Visualization 
● On Booking Detail: Button “Host a game from this slot”. 
● Host-led Game Creation Sheet: 
○ Step 1: Confirm date/time/sport/venue (prefilled). 
○ Step 2: Set number of extra players and any notes. 
○ Step 3: Publish → appears on Matches feed. 
6. Cross-cutting Requirements 
6.1 Performance 
● All key pages (Home, Venues list, Venue detail) should load within 2–3 seconds on average 4G. 
6.2 Security & Privacy 
● Phone number and personal details stored securely. 
● Payment handled via PCI-compliant gateways; no sensitive card data stored directly. 
6.3 Analytics & KPIs 
Track: 
● Time from app open → booking confirmation (median). 
● Conversion funnel:
○ Onboarding complete 
○ Venue viewed 
○ Slot selected 
○ Payment started 
○ Payment success 
● Engagement: 
○ DAU/MAU 
○ Matches joined/created per user 
● Monetization: 
○ Average discount per booking 
○ Credits earned vs redeemed 
These align with the broader KPIs like conversion time and retention rate in the strategic blueprint. 
7. Out of Scope (for This MVP) 
● Full player statistics and advanced gamification (badges, levels beyond simple XP). ● Corporate tournament management suite. 
● Equipment marketplace. 
● AI-driven matchmaking or national analytics dashboards.
